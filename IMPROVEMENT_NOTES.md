# Draft review and design decisions

## What worked in the Squarespace draft

- Strong gray, black, and bright-green brand system.
- Clear headline and a credible founder story.
- Real project photography instead of generic car-detailing imagery.
- Photo-estimate form is a strong low-friction conversion path.
- The three service offers provide useful price anchors.

## Main improvements made

1. **Reordered the home page around the customer decision.** The technician hero, problem-focused message, and free estimate now appear before the product still life.
2. **Reduced the header height.** The original gray header consumed too much of the first screen and did not clearly expose Services or Results.
3. **Replaced the oversized ticker.** The brand phrase remains, but it no longer cuts off most of the text or dominate the page.
4. **Created separate pages.** Services, Results, About, and Contact now have their own URLs for clearer navigation and local SEO.
5. **Made service cards scannable.** Long bullet lists are moved to the Services page, while the home page keeps only the clearest entry points.
6. **Rebuilt the gallery as case-oriented cards.** Every image has a descriptive caption and opens at full size on the Results page.
7. **Shortened testimonials.** The original three-column wall of text was difficult to read; the new version uses strong excerpts.
8. **Added trust and process content.** Photo-first estimates, realistic-result language, mobile-service explanation, and the three-step process answer common objections.
9. **Improved mobile behavior and accessibility.** Responsive navigation, readable type sizes, keyboard focus, alt text, reduced-motion support, and semantic form labels are included.
10. **Prepared the site for GitHub Pages.** The package includes metadata, favicon, manifest, sitemap, robots file, CNAME, 404 page, and deployment instructions.
