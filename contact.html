Before sending

Three photos make a better estimate.

Use indirect daylight when possible. Clean loose dirt from the area so scratches, transfer, or dents are visible.

Is the estimate final?

It is an initial estimate based on the photos and information provided. Final pricing may change after physical inspection if the damage, material, or prior repair differs from what is visible.

Do you offer mobile service?

Yes, many services can be completed on-site. Larger restoration work may require a different setup; we confirm the best option before scheduling.

Can every scratch be removed?

No. Some defects penetrate beyond the clear coat or paint layer. In those cases, the goal may be to reduce visibility, protect the area, or recommend repainting.

How quickly will I hear back?

Most requests can be reviewed once clear photos and vehicle details are available. For urgent scheduling, call or text directly.
